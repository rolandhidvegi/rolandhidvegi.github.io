    CanSat

    This is the PCB I am most proud of.
    It is the main PCB of the CanSat that participated in the 2023 edition of RCRC. It contains a lot of things tightly packed together, such as 2 synchronous step-down switch mode converters for 3.3V and 5V, a microcontroller, a LoRa Module, a microSD conector, and a bunch of sensors: temperature, humidity, pressure, and a 6-axis IMU, CO2 sensor, and a GPS module.
