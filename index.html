    Projects

    CanSat

    This is the PCB I am most proud of.
    It is the main PCB of the CanSat that participated in the 2023 edition of RCRC. It contains a lot of things tightly packed together, such as 2 synchronous step-down switch mode converters for 3.3V and 5V, a microcontroller, a LoRa Module, a microSD conector, and a bunch of sensors: temperature, humidity, pressure, a 6-axis IMU, CO2, as well as connectivity for a GPS module, GSM module, RS485 soil sensor, 4 analog inputs for SO2, NO2, CO, UV light and and solar irradiance.
